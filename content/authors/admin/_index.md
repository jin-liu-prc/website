I am a Research Fellow at the University of Melbourne and a physical oceanographer working on ocean waves, climate variability and coastal change. I combine numerical modelling, observations and data analysis to investigate coastal hazards, marine renewable energy and nature-based coastal protection.

My current research focuses on wave–mangrove interactions and hybrid living shorelines, including SWAN and XBeach modelling and field measurements in New South Wales. Previously, I worked as a Science Officer with the Victoria State Government and contributed to the Victorian Coastal Monitoring Program.

I hold a PhD in Physical Oceanography from the University of Melbourne, an MSc from Zhejiang University, and a BSc from Guangdong Ocean University.
