How this map is counted and sourced

The publication list comes from my Google Scholar profile. Countries follow institutional addresses in available publication records, rather than authors’ nationalities or their current workplaces. Each person is counted once per country across all years; a person with affiliations in multiple countries can appear in each. Jin Liu is excluded.

Only verified affiliations are counted. Unresolved records are retained for review. Country markers represent UN member states with verified collaborators; they do not indicate research field sites. The basemap uses Natural Earth public-domain data.
